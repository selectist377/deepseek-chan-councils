Councils (deepseek-chan edition) 4.5

Extract the archive and open index.html to play. No npm, Node.js, or local server required.
For English, open index.en.html, or use the language button at the lower-left.

Folders:
  index.html / index.en.html   Chinese / English entry
  characters/                  Seven portraits and card art
  pets/, pets-all/             Pet actions and codex
  music/                       Background music
  sw.js, manifest*.webmanifest Offline cache and PWA manifests

The mobile layout is compact. The pet can be dragged anywhere and remembers its position; hide it and restore it from the pet button.
Host the whole folder at the root of a GitHub Pages repository.
